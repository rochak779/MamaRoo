# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what MamaRoo collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

To provide the app, we collect the profile details you give us (such as your name
and due date), the pregnancy dates you enter, the medicines and appointments you
log, the doctor's advice notes you add, the vitals you record, any report files
you upload, the text of your daily check-ins, and the messages you send to the
chat assistant.

## Where your data lives

Your records, including report files, are stored with Supabase in the Mumbai
region. Everything stays in India unless this policy is updated to say
otherwise.

## Voice input

If you use voice to add a check-in, the recording is transcribed to text on your
own device. The audio itself is never uploaded to our servers and never stored.
Only the text you see before you submit it is kept.

## The chat assistant and AI providers

When you ask the chat assistant a question, your question text and the reviewed
reference passages it draws on are sent to an outside AI provider to generate a
response. Nothing from your saved records, such as your vitals, medicines or
appointments, is included in that request.

Before your question is sent, the app removes anything that looks like an email
address, a phone number, or a name stored in your own profile. This is a
best-effort check, not a guarantee: it cannot catch everything you might type. A
street address, another person's name, a medicine name, or an alphanumeric
identifier included in your question may still reach the provider. Avoid typing
anything you would not want an outside service to see.

## Analytics

We use product analytics to understand how the app is used, hosted outside
India. This is opt-in and off by default. You can turn it on or off at any time
in Settings. Analytics events never include health information or anything you
typed. They record only counts, categories and simple yes or no flags.

## Your rights

You can export a copy of your own data from Settings at any time. You can also
delete your account from Settings. This permanently removes your account and
every record linked to it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@mamaroo.app.
